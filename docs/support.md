# Glitter Live Support

## Set a live wallpaper

Apps can't set the wallpaper themselves, so the last step happens in Photos:

1. Open **Photos** and find the Live Photo you saved from Glitter Live.
2. Tap **Share**, then choose **Use as Wallpaper**.
3. Make sure the **Live Photo** button in the bottom corner is on.
4. Tap **Add**, then **Set as Wallpaper Pair**.

Your wallpaper moves briefly each time you wake your iPhone. These steps are also in the app under Settings → How to set a live wallpaper.

## Common questions

**The wallpaper editor says "Motion Not Available".**
Check that the Live Photo button is on in the wallpaper editor. Short clips work best: trim to 1–3 seconds in Glitter Live and save again.

**The wallpaper doesn't move.**
Motion plays when you wake your iPhone, not while the Lock Screen is already showing. Live wallpapers stay still in Low Power Mode, so turn it off and try again.

**Where can I get videos from?**
Photos, Files, a direct link to a video file (including Dropbox and Google Drive share links), or by sharing a video to Glitter Live from another app. Videos on sites like YouTube and Instagram can't be downloaded from a link; if it's your own video, save it to Photos from that app first.

**Can Glitter Live see my photos?**
No. It only asks to *add* the Live Photos you create, and can't see anything else in your library. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

**Is it free?**
Explore is free. Your first 5 conversions are free too. After that, each conversion is free with a short ad, or a one-time Unlimited Conversions purchase removes the limit and the ads. There's never a watermark.

**I bought Unlimited Conversions on another iPhone.**
Open Settings in Glitter Live and tap **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account.

**How do I change my ad choices?**
Tracking is under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU, UK and similar regions, Glitter Live also has Settings → Ad privacy choices. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
