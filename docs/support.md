# Glitter Live Support

## Set a live wallpaper

Apps can't set the wallpaper themselves, so the last step happens in Photos:

1. Open **Photos** and find the Live Photo you saved from Glitter Live.
2. Tap **Share**, then choose **Use as Wallpaper**.
3. Make sure the **Live Photo** button in the bottom corner is on.
4. Tap **Add**, then **Set as Wallpaper Pair**.

Your wallpaper moves briefly each time you wake your iPhone.

## Common questions

**The wallpaper editor says "Motion Not Available".**
Check that the Live Photo button is on in the wallpaper editor. Short clips work best: trim to 1–3 seconds in Glitter Live and save again.

**The wallpaper doesn't move.**
Motion plays when you wake your iPhone, not while the Lock Screen is already showing. Low Power Mode can also stop it.

**Can Glitter Live see my photos?**
No. It only asks to *add* the Live Photos you create, and can't see anything else in your library. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

**Is it free?**
Yes. Converting videos and saving wallpapers from Explore are free, with no watermark.
