# Glitter Live Privacy Policy

_Effective 3 October 2026_

Glitter Live turns videos into Live Photo wallpapers. Your videos and photos never leave your iPhone. The app has no account. The only data that leaves the device is what the ads need, and only when you choose to watch one.

## Your videos and photos

- **Videos you convert stay on your iPhone.** Trimming, framing and creating the Live Photo all happen on the device. Your videos are never uploaded.
- **Photos access is add-only.** Glitter Live asks only for permission to *add* the Live Photos you create to your library. It can't see, read or change anything else in Photos.
- **Files and links.** Videos you open from Files are copied into the app to edit. Videos you open from a link are downloaded straight from that link to your iPhone; we don't see the link or the video.
- **Your Library lives on your iPhone.** The wallpapers you make are kept inside the app so you can preview, save or delete them. Deleting a wallpaper in the app, or deleting the app, removes them. Copies you saved to Photos stay there until you delete them in Photos.

## Ads

Your first 5 conversions are free. After that, you can watch a short ad to save each wallpaper, or unlock unlimited conversions with a one-time purchase. Ads only appear when you choose to watch one.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects:

- a device identifier, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
- your approximate location, estimated from your IP address
- information about the ads you see and how you interact with them
- performance and crash data from the ad SDK

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services, as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices**

- **Tracking.** The first time you watch an ad, iOS asks whether Glitter Live may track you across other apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier isn't shared, and you may see less relevant ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Consent in the EU, UK and similar regions.** Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ad loads. You can change them later in Glitter Live under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
- **No ads at all.** The one-time Unlimited Conversions purchase removes ads from Convert.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether Unlimited Conversions has been bought, so it can be restored on your other devices.

## Explore

The wallpapers in Explore are downloaded from our catalog, which is hosted by [Supabase](https://supabase.com). As with any download, the request includes technical details such as your IP address, which the hosting provider may log briefly to run the service. We don't use these details to identify you, and nothing about what you browse or save is sent back to us.

## Children

Glitter Live isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.
